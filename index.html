Daily Attendance Sheet

Monthly learner attendance register based on DepEd School Form 2 (SF2). Saved records are shared with linked parent accounts.

Monthly attendance record

Loading students...

No students in this class

Add or import students before recording attendance.

Select P (Present), A (Absent), T (Tardy), or E (Excused) for each school day. Add a reason whenever a learner is marked absent; linked parents can see it in their portal. Use Monthly Remarks to record NLS, Transferred Out, or Transferred In and choose the exact status date; these adjust the SF2 Registered Learner as of End of the Month count. NLS and Transferred Out lock attendance from the following month onward until the status changes; learners stay listed and are excluded from attendance totals. Dates marked Holiday/No Class display NC for every learner and are excluded from class-day totals.
